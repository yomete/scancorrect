## Recording Tips

### What to actually record:

1. **Prepare test files** — Get 36 real scans with Epson/Plustek metadata
2. **Clean desktop** — Hide personal files, use a neutral wallpaper
3. **Slow your cursor** — Move deliberately, pause on key UI elements
4. **Use a real catalog** — If possible, show a Lightroom catalog with hundreds of "scanner" entries to make the problem feel real
5. **Record at 2x size** — Record at 2560x1440 minimum so you can crop/zoom in post

### What NOT to show:
- Don't show the Settings/preferences (boring)
- Don't show error states (save for docs)
- Don't dwell on the profile list if you only have 1-2 profiles
